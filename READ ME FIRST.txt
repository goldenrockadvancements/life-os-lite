Life OS Lite - an Obsidian vault by Golden Rock
================================================================

1. Install Obsidian (free): https://obsidian.md
2. Unzip this file. Move the "Life OS Lite" folder somewhere you keep documents
   (not inside another vault).
3. Open Obsidian > "Open folder as vault" > pick the "Life OS Lite" folder.
4. The Home note opens. Read "Start Here" first (5 minutes).

No community plugins are needed, so Obsidian's "Restricted mode" can stay on.
Works on Windows, Mac, Linux, iPhone, iPad and Android.
