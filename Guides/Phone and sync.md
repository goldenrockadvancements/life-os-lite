Your vault is a normal folder of text files. Pick **one** way to get it onto your other devices:

| Option | Cost | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| **Obsidian Sync** | Paid plan from Obsidian | The easiest. End-to-end encrypted, works on every device, keeps version history. |
| **iCloud Drive** | Free with Apple ID | iPhone/iPad + Mac. Create the vault inside *iCloud Drive > Obsidian*. |
| **Syncthing / Möbius Sync** | Free | Tinkerers. Device-to-device, no cloud. |
| **Git** | Free | Developers. Gives full history. |

> [!warning] Don't mix sync methods
> Running two sync tools on the same vault (say iCloud **and** Dropbox) is the most common way people lose notes. Pick one.

On the phone, everything in this vault works the same: bases, templates, daily notes. Nothing needs a plugin.
