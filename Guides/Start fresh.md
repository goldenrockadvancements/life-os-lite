The vault ships with **sample notes** so every base and dashboard shows something on day one. When you're ready to use it for real:

1. Open [[Sample data.base]]. It lists every note tagged `#sample`.
2. Select them all in the file list (they live in 01 Inbox, 02 Tasks) and delete them. Or delete them one by one as you replace them with your own.
3. Keep the **Templates**, **Bases**, **Guides** and **Home**: those are the system.

> [!tip] Not ready to delete?
> Leave the samples for a week and copy their patterns. A sample note is the fastest way to see what a good note looks like.
