Your head is for having ideas, not for holding them.

1. Open the Inbox: new note in `01 Inbox` with the **Capture** template (or just a line in today's daily note).
2. Write one thing per note or line. Tasks, worries, ideas, "call mom", all of it.
3. Don't organize. Don't do anything yet. Just write until your head feels quieter.

Then, when you have a bit of energy, [[Sort the inbox]].
