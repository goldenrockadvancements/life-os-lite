Welcome to **Life OS Lite**, the free edition of [Life OS](https://goldenrockadvancements.com/vaults/life-os/). It's a calm Today page: a brain-dump inbox, and small tasks you can pick by **energy** and **minutes**.

## Start in 3 minutes
1. Do a [[Brain dump in 2 minutes|2-minute brain dump]] into `01 Inbox`.
2. Turn a few items into tasks with the **Task** template: set *energy* and *minutes*.
3. Tick **focus** on up to 3 tasks, and set one to **Doing**. See [[Pick your 3]].
4. Clear the fictional samples when you like: [[Start fresh]].

Life OS is a planning tool, not a treatment. It doesn't diagnose anything.
