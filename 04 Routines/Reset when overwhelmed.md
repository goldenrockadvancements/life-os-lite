---
type: routine
when: As needed
minutes: 10
---
For when everything feels like too much. Ten minutes, in order. Stop after any step if you feel better.

1. **Pause.** Drink some water. Three slow breaths.
2. **Empty your head.** Write everything on your mind into the Inbox, one line each. Don't sort, just write. See [[Brain dump in 2 minutes]].
3. **Pick one thing.** The smallest, easiest task you can see. If nothing is small enough, make it smaller: "open the email" counts.
4. **Set a timer for 10 minutes** and do just that one thing.
5. **Done is enough.** Mark it done. If you want, pick the next one. If not, that was a win.

If overwhelm is constant or heavy, talking to a doctor, counselor or someone you trust can help. You don't have to handle it alone.
